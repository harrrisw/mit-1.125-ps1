Definitions, units & calculations

All amounts are nominal US dollars. “Total project budgets” sums Total_Project_Budget: full project costs, including prior years and future funds, not just FY27–31 expenditure. FY2027 runs July 1, 2026–June 30, 2027.

“Reported spending” sums GO_Expended, OC_Expended and Grant_Expended through FY2025. External-fund expenditure is not supplied. The gap between budget and spending is not an overrun or delay measure.

Funding sums Existing + FY + Future fields for City capital (Authorization), other City (OC), and grants (Grant), plus External_Funds. All 325 funding totals reconcile to their project budgets. Status labels are retained as published; “In construction” counts only that exact status.

Department and neighborhood charts sum lifetime budgets. Comparisons use the entire dataset, while the dashboard and project table share filters. Search also filters dashboard totals. Percentages use the currently selected total, except the explicitly citywide findings. Map colors use a square-root scale to make smaller allocations visible; the legend rescales to the current selection. Gray means zero selected budget or no matching budget label.

Geography & what the map means

The map shows neighborhood allocations, not exact construction sites. Budget records have no coordinates. Allston and Brighton share the combined “Allston / Brighton” total; Downtown maps to “Downtown / Government Center,” Fenway to “Fenway / Kenmore,” and both South Boston polygons share “South Boston.” Shared values are never added twice in totals.

Longwood and Leather District have no matching budget label and appear gray. This does not imply no investment. Citywide and Multiple Neighborhoods records remain in totals but are not spread across polygons. Harbor Islands are shown in an inset at a different scale.

Missing data, bias & uncertainty

The City overview lists 321 projects and a $4.4 billion plan; this downloaded adopted table contains 325 unique IDs totaling $4,470,029,155. We report the table exactly and do not force a match. Differences may reflect release timing or scope; they have not been reconciled with the City.

One source-quality example: CCC25010 is named “BCYF Grove Hall Community Center” but assigned to Hyde Park. We retain both published fields and flag that record. This cautions against treating neighborhood labels as verified site addresses.

The plan does not provide exact street addresses or coordinates, verified completion dates, procurement contracts, contractor payments, asset condition, population denominators, or outcome measures. It is not a complete inventory of already-built infrastructure. Spending and status have different time cutoffs. A low spending share cannot establish delay.

Large citywide programs and expensive assets can dominate totals. Dollars alone do not measure fairness, resident benefit, or unmet need. State, MBTA, federal, and other independently financed infrastructure is outside this municipal plan unless included in a project record. No inflation adjustment is applied.
